Who We Are

Beyond academia. Beyond policy. Into real community action.

Transforming Knowledge into Impact

Beyond the Science (BTS) is a Ghanaian-based NGO working at the intersection of research, policy, creativity, and community life.

We combine local knowledge, lived experiences, and innovative tools such as VR, storytelling, games, and theatre to make climate action inclusive and practical.

Our Belief

Effective climate solutions happen when evidence meets people, creativity meets policy, and communities lead change.

Our Pillars

Three interconnected pathways to sustainable futures.

Knowledge to Action

Training, mentorship, and co-design of locally-led climate solutions with youth, women, and communities.

Innovative Dissemination

VR, games, theatre, podcasts, films, and visual tools that turn complex data into clear stories.

ESG & Policy Advisory

Supporting institutions and businesses with climate governance, sustainability strategies, and ESG integration.
