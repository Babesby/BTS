Work across the evidence cycle.

Six connected programme areas create different routes from knowledge to action.

01 / Influence

Evidence to Policy & Action

Research, public institutions and communities shaping practical decisions together.
02 / Connect

Climate and Development Knowledge Network

Locally led knowledge, leadership and collaboration for climate-resilient development.
03 / Adapt

Climate Adaptation & Resilience

Evidence and co-creation tools that strengthen institutional adaptation.
04 / Enterprise

Entrepreneurship & Commercial Business

Green enterprise, ESG practice and climate-conscious commercial action.
05 / Communicate

Evidence to Public Awareness

Broadcasting and storytelling that bring climate evidence into everyday life.
06 / Learn

Innovative Climate Literacy

Digital, immersive and game-based learning that makes climate knowledge accessible.
